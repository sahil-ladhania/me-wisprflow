# ScannedDocument

A scan of a real object (an email, a certificate, a screenshot, a first program) placed on the page as proof, with one red-pen mark pointing at what matters.

**Provide:** the scan as an image inside `.rd-scan__sheet` (or recreated rows for legibility on phones), a kicker label ("SCAN 02 · THE INTERVIEW INVITE"), the date, an optional red-pen circle (`__circle`) around the proof and a short red-pen note (`__note`).

**Rules**
- Real objects only. Never mock up another company's branding or a record that didn't happen; blur other people's names and addresses.
- One red-pen mark per scan: a circle or an underline, plus at most six words of note.
- Tilt ≤1.5deg. Scans sit on `scan` (slightly greyer than paper) with a faint scanner texture, so they read as copies.
- Make the proof legible without zooming: crop to the line that matters, and put the full scan behind a tap.
