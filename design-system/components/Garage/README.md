# Garage

The final stop and the footer: the bike parked, proof that every line was dictated through Wispr Flow, the bloopers, the contact, and the sign pointing to the café in the mountains.

**Provide:** the headline, a dictation log (`.rd-log`: timestamp + line, real mishearings as inline Corrections), two counters (real values from Wispr Flow's stats), contact links, and the final `Signboard`.

**Rules**
- Bloopers are real and verbatim; the example lines show the format. The "rolling field" one leads.
- Every blooper is struck in red pen with the right words after it (`.rd-fix--inline`), so the garage repeats the site's honesty device.
- "N,NNN" is a placeholder: fill with the real count or remove the counter. Never invent numbers.
- Links are plain underlined text. "Résumé (if you insist)" is the only place a résumé appears.
- The last thing on the page is the green sign. No "Thanks for visiting", no back-to-top rocket.
