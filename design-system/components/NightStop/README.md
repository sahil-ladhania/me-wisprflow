# NightStop

The June 2025 stop, the only one in the night theme: one straight, honest line about Sahil's pain and growth, in silence.

**Provide:** `data-theme="night"` on the section (every token switches underneath), the date, the one line, and optionally a quiet `VoiceNote`. The example line shows the register only; Sahil writes his own.

**Rules**
- One sentence. No wit, no italic, no strike-throughs, no photos, no tape, no stats, no highlight, no red pen.
- About his pain and his growth only. Never name, describe or blame the other person.
- Smoke transition in and out (see Motion). The only light is the bike's headlamp, low in the frame.
- `night-line` style: Newsreader Light, centred, max 24ch, with `space-9` of silence above and below.
- The stops on either side keep their jokes off the screen edge adjacent to this one.
