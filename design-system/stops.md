## Rules for the map

- Past years: `haldi` milestone caps. 2026 "now": `ink` cap. Next and someday: `signboard` caps.
- Voice notes at five stops at most, never two in a row except 9 → 10.
- The two loudest stops (lulu and Wispr Flow) carry the numbers. The Night stop carries none.
- Every stop's title must work alone: read just the titles in order and the story should still land.
