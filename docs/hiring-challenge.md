# Wispr Flow Hiring Challenge

## Key facts
- Link: https://wispr-seven-day-challenge.wispr-flow-5934.chatgpt.site/
- Deadline: 4 Nov 2026
- Run by Wispr Flow together with Emergent (a vibe-coding tool). Emergent credits are given out.

## Why this challenge exists
- Wispr Flow is a voice dictation app on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.
- They are building a growth team in India.
- 2,000+ people applied for their internship through Nimisha's LinkedIn post.
- Everyone sent the same over-polished PDF resume. Neither the team nor their AI could tell people apart.
- So they replaced the resume with this challenge.

## The brief
- "Your first brief is the subject you know best: YOU."
- Build an interactive window into:
  - the chances you took
  - the mistakes you learned from
  - what you care about
  - how you think
- Build it with your voice: speak into the build tool through Wispr Flow.
- The FAQ allows vibe-coding tools other than Emergent.
- Their belief: more of you comes through when you speak than in a standard resume.
- Suggested formats: interactive site, choose-your-own-path story, browser game, AMA or mock-interview app, interactive timeline, simulator, quiz, or something new.

## Submission form
- Name
- Wispr Flow account email (used to verify voice use)
- Role interest (dropdown) and earliest start date
- Portfolio URL: must be new, built for this challenge, and public. Old portfolios are rejected.
- Recording URL: 3 min max. Covers what you built, why, and what value voice added.
- LinkedIn profile
- "Oddly specific setup requirement": what you'd spend the $3,000 setup grant on
- Permission to feature the build publicly (yes/no)
- Optional: post it on LinkedIn or X and tag Wispr Flow and Emergent ("Every campaign starts with a post")

## How it is judged
| Criterion | What they look for |
|---|---|
| Creativity | How creatively you show who you are and what you can do |
| Use of voice | How voice brings your ideas to life and what it adds to building |
| Extreme ownership | What you owned, the decisions you made, what you learned |
| Work over credentials | How you think and what you've made happen |

- The FAQ adds: clarity in the video, understanding of voice, ability to build independently, creative flair on an ordinary task.
- You are judged relative to your experience.
- Full-time applicants must also show past ownership.
- Voice use is verified through the recording and through Flow account activity during the challenge.

## The roles
- Main hire: a couple of interns for the India growth team.
- Full-time: own channels like influencer marketing and partnerships. That means deciding experiments, building relationships, launching campaigns, measuring results. Needs past ownership of a channel at a tech startup.
- Requirements: full-time availability, Bangalore-based or willing to travel, ideal start in November.
- Offer: top-of-market pay, a $3,000 setup grant, "crazy campaigns in India", and a path to full-time for interns.

## What this means for the build
- This is a growth role, not engineering. The build must prove growth and campaign thinking, not just code.
- Voice must be visible in the process and inside the product, not a footnote.
- The optional launch post is a free test of campaign skill. Treat it as part of the submission.
- Polished but generic loses. It has to be memorable and true.
- No faking, padding or inflated numbers.
- Not a sales pitch. Show who Sahil is, how he thinks, and why he'd be useful to Wispr's India growth.

## Open risk
- The form says "built on Emergent", but the FAQ allows other tools. Sahil is building with Claude Code, dictating every prompt through Wispr Flow. Mention the tool choice clearly in the video.
